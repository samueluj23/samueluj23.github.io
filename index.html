About

I co-founded rigitiX, Africa's event OS for ticketing, door, promoters and settlement. We took it from about 3k organic users in one city to 5 cities, without paid acquisition.

I own positioning, go-to-market, content, community, partnerships and organic acquisition, and work with cross-functional teams of 10+. Alongside rigitiX, I'm Zerion's ambassador and Nigeria growth lead.

Before that I ran growth, community and partnerships for Web3 and AI products: GameGPT, Dyleum, Wondera and others. I like work where the audience turns into users, revenue or distribution.
